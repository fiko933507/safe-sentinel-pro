Safe Sentinel Pro — Privacy Policy

Safe Sentinel Pro provides crypto-wallet security, blockchain risk analysis, scam/threat intelligence, wallet monitoring, security notifications and related tools. The app is designed to be non-custodial and should not request or store private keys or seed phrases.

Data processed

Depending on the features used, Safe Sentinel Pro may process name, email address, password hash, authentication/security logs, IP/user-agent, wallet addresses, blockchain network/transaction information, whitelist/blacklist/Vault/Whale Watch records, Guardian settings, inheritance-protocol addresses, price alerts, security notifications and VIP subscription/payment-verification transaction IDs.

Purposes

Data may be used for account creation and authentication, requested wallet/blockchain analysis, security and fraud prevention, user-enabled monitoring, security alerts, VIP payment verification, service reliability and troubleshooting.

Third-party services

Wallet/contract addresses or blockchain query parameters may be sent to RPC/API providers to deliver requested blockchain functionality. The app may also use third-party market-data and wallet-connection services/SDKs, which may process technical data under their own privacy policies.

Security

The production architecture uses password hashing, secure mobile token storage where supported, HTTPS-only production networking, rate limiting, security headers, CORS controls and input validation. Server secrets must not be embedded in the mobile client.

Account and data deletion

Users can start account deletion through the in-app Permanently Delete Account option. The flow is designed to remove the account and associated application records. Certain security events may be retained in anonymized/de-linked form or limited records may be retained where required by law or security obligations. Transactions already recorded on a public blockchain cannot be erased by Safe Sentinel Pro.
